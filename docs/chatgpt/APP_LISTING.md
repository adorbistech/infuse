## Detailed Description

**INFUSE** is the universal execution intelligence platform and real-time voltage regulator for AI agents.

Connect INFUSE to ChatGPT to inspect multi-agent executions, track live token costs, analyze health degradation across AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, DeepSeek), and enforce active governance policies.

### Key Capabilities

1. **Observe & Inspect Trajectories**: Query live and historical execution runs across your autonomous agent fleet (Claude Code, OpenCode, Codex, Hermes, Lovable).
2. **State & Anomaly Detection**: Read real-time canonical states (`NORMAL`, `COST_PRESSURE`, `RUNAWAY`, `QUALITY_DEGRADED`, `PROVIDER_CONSTRAINED`).
3. **Governor Decision Insights**: View the Governor's regulatory actions (`CONTINUE`, `OPTIMIZE`, `ESCALATE`, `DOWNGRADE`, `SWITCH`, `THROTTLE`, `STOP`) with machine-readable taxonomy reason codes.
4. **Governed Execution**: Dispatch AI tasks through the multi-provider routing matrix with built-in token budgets and safety boundaries.
5. **Active Interventions**: Safely throttle, switch providers, or stop runaway executions directly from ChatGPT.
